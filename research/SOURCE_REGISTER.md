# Primary sources for the single-problem research programme

- **Mishra v1**, Tapas Kumar Mishra, *Erdős Rado Sunflower (Conjecture) Theorem*, **1 June 2026**, https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02667v1 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02667v1 . Section 3 asserts a full-shift quantitative sunflower-number bound `tau(S(F)) <= 3 tau(F)^2`; Section 7 derives `f(k,s) <= (3s^2)^(6s^2-2) 2^k`. The latter is **FALSE FOR s>=4** by classical (s-1)^k lower bound, independently of the validity of intervening lemmas. Section 3's unique one-element original-source attribution is not generally valid after multiple compressions. These are the research gap, **not verified theorems**.
- **Mishra v2**, Tapas Kumar Mishra, *Erdős Rado Sunflower Theorem for Shifted Families*, **8 June 2026**, https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02667v2 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02667v2 . Restricted `f'(k,s)` theorem and recurrence; Section 3 proves a one-shift bound `tau(C_ij(F))<=2 tau(F)` (not a ground-set-independent full-shift bound). Section 6 claims `f'(k,s) <= s^(2s-2) 2^k`. Treat as source claims to audit, not automatic full proof.
- **Bell–Chueluecha–Warnke**, *Note on Sunflowers*, Discrete Mathematics 344 (2021), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2021.112367 . Reliable published general upper baseline `(C s log k)^k` (up to parameter conventions); not necessarily exhaustive best unrefereed-preprint result in October 2026.
- **Classical Erdős–Rado** lower products: `P(s-1,k)` chooses one of s-1 disjoint labelled vertices in each of k coordinate blocks; it is s-sunflower-free and has `(s-1)^k` edges. Mandatory consistency check for ANY claimed bound.
- **Status caveat:** arXiv v1 full conjecture abstract was superseded by v2 restricted abstract. Full conjecture remains an open research target under available independent-source evidence, not certified impossible or solved. More prior art is welcome when it changes actual mathematical obligations.

All dates are version dates; source claims are not independently peer-reviewed or newly discovered programme results.
