# SUN-PARENT-S007 — primary source register and priority correction (2026-10-14)

**Status:** targeted live source check; NOT exhaustive independent priority search and NOT referee validation.

1. **Ge–Wang–Xu–Zhao**, *Bounded VC-dimension implies the Erdős–Rado sunflower conjecture*, **2026 arXiv preprint** [2609.18995](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18995), inspected primary HTML Theorem 1.1 and proof outline: for d≥1,r≥2,ℓ≥1, an ℓ-uniform family VC≤d of size greater than `(50dr)^ℓ` has an r-sunflower; source also states ℓ-bounded sum `Σ_{j=0}^ℓ (50dr)^j`. Publication/referee status NOT independently certified. **PRIORITY KILL**: proposing a fixed-VC-dimension constant-base theorem (including d=2) as novel is invalid. This supersedes the S004 candidate #5 literature baseline, which cited only the weaker 2025 `(Cr(log d+log* ℓ))^ℓ`. The source's arXiv identifier and submission metadata display a date inconsistency; attribution and mathematical statement rather than an inferred priority date govern.
2. **Balogh–Bernshteyn–Delcourt–Ferber–Pham**, *Sunflowers in Set Systems with Small VC-Dimension*, [Combinatorica 45 (2025), article 58](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00493-025-00186-8), published Theorems 1.2 and 1.4: VC≤d bound `(Cr(log d+log* ℓ))^ℓ` and sharp VC=1 bound `(r−1)^ℓ`. Now **historical baseline**, not state-of-art fixed-d.
3. **Bell–Chueluecha–Warnke**, *Note on Sunflowers*, [Discrete Mathematics 344 (2021), 112367](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2021.112367), general unconditional upper `(C r log ℓ)^ℓ` (constant C), from the Rao/ALWZ line; **all-rank log gap still unresolved**. [ALWZ 2019/2021 arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08483) robust version is quantitatively different and its sharpness does not itself settle ordinary Erdős–Rado.
4. **Rao**, *The Story of Sunflowers*, [JLMS 113 (2026)](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1112/jlms.70380), published open-problem survey; unrestricted all-r constant-base status not superseded by the bounded-VC preprint.
5. **Naslund–Sawin**, *Upper Bounds for Sunflower-Free Sets*, [Forum Math. Sigma 5 (2017) e15](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2017.12): `U(D,n) ≤ [3 (D−1)^(2/3)/2^(2/3)]^n`; journal Thm 2 vs arXiv v1 Thm 5. A uniform `D^(2/3−δ)` estimate is NOT a D-independent constant-base bound. **Alon–Shpilka–Umans** [Comput. Complexity (2013), Thm 2.6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00037-013-0060-1) transfers D-independent 3-word bound to ordinary r=3 only, not arbitrary petal count.
6. **Janzer–Jin–Sudakov–Wu**, [Combinatorica 46 (2026), article 29](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00493-026-00222-1): restricted intersection and original-family sunflower extraction sources for frozen P001A. Does NOT supply final-H internal-valid extraction or a rank-uniform exponential c_k.
7. **Axante–Budala–Chitic–Dumitru–Nacu**, [arXiv:2609.06175v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06175) (2026 preprint): `39 ≤ f(3,4) ≤ 49`; source-assisted upper remains not independently certificate-replayed by programme; no P001B global improvement.
8. **Cavalar–Fabris–Mukhopadhyay–Srinivasan–Yehudayoff**, [ECCC TR26-220](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/220/) (28 September 2026) already **PROVES O(w)-wise-independent robust sunflower derandomisation**, albeit with changed quantitative parameters. Therefore suggesting O(w²)→O(w) as an original result is also PRIOR ART. This concerns robust/algorithmic sunflowers, not an ordinary `C_r^k` theorem.

Primary-source statement checked ≠ proof independently refereed. No hit ≠ novelty clearance. All these results belong to their source authors.
