# Novelty in direct research

The sole immediate aim is a correct proof of the full conjecture or a substantial rigorous mathematical advance toward it. Attribute all sourced facts; do not claim originality merely because GitHub tests pass or a search finds no hit. Check relevant mathematics when a substantive positive theorem is obtained. No novelty bureaucracy or paper milestone quotas before proof work. Maintain a clear distinction between historical v1/v2 paper claims and genuinely new mathematics.
