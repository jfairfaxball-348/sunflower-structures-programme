# Research charter (updated 2026-10-14)

Research the unrestricted Erdős–Rado Sunflower Conjecture with the goal of proving it, using Mishra 2606.02667 v1/v2 as the starting mathematical pivot. This is a single, long-running research effort in one repo; no portfolio of paper projects, subsidiary repositories or session-count driven decisions. Historic files are preserved but not controlling. Priority is new mathematical insight and rigorous proof, not throughput or publication staging.
